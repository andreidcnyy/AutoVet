                            PART I - INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ABOUT AUTOVET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AutoVet is a veterinary clinic management system made up of two connected
applications that share one database:

  (a) THE CLIENT PORTAL
      A website for pet owners. Owners register their pets, book visits,
      track medical summaries, view invoices, receive reminders and leave
      reviews.

  (b) THE CLINIC ADMIN SYSTEM
      The staff workspace. Staff manage patient records, medical records,
      appointments, walk-ins, inventory, invoicing, users and clinic
      settings.

The system also includes intelligent modules:

  * AI INVENTORY FORECASTING - predicts when stock will run out using a
    regression model trained on the clinic's own consumption history, and
    flags items as Low Stock before they are depleted.
  * AI CLINICAL SUPPORT - generates supporting suggestions from a patient's
    profile, history and the symptoms a veterinarian types in. It is a
    reference aid only; it does NOT diagnose or prescribe.
  * VIRTUAL ASSISTANT - a rule-based chatbot on the portal that answers
    frequently asked questions about location, hours, services and booking.

Changes made in one application appear in the other. Several screens update
live over a realtime connection (for example, an appointment approval appears
on the owner's screen without a page refresh).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHO USES THE SYSTEM (USER ROLES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
There are five roles. What you can see and do depends on your role.

  OWNER (Pet Owner)
      Uses the Client Portal only. Manages own pets, bookings, invoices and
      profile. Cannot see other owners' data.

  STAFF
      Clinic Admin System. Day-to-day operations: dashboard, analytics,
      patients, appointments, inventory, invoices. No access to Maintenance
      (Settings) or Reviews management.

  VETERINARIAN
      Everything Staff can do, plus Reviews & Feedback, Maintenance
      (Settings), and clinical actions such as writing medical records.

  CLINIC ADMIN
      Full control of one clinic: everything above, plus user management,
      master data, backups, archives and audit logs.

  SUPER ADMIN
      Platform level. Manages clinics, creates clinic administrators,
      publishes system-wide broadcasts and reviews system logs. Does NOT
      work inside a clinic's day-to-day patient data.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS & ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * A modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox or Safari,
    updated to a recent version).
  * An internet connection.
  * The Client Portal works on phones, tablets and desktops.
  * The Clinic Admin System is designed for desktop or laptop screens; it
    works on a tablet but a larger screen is strongly recommended.
  * Owners need a working email address (a verification link is sent to it).
  * Staff accounts are created by a Clinic Admin - staff cannot self-register.

  Your clinic will give you the two web addresses:
      Client Portal        -> used by pet owners
      Clinic Admin System  -> used by clinic personnel

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. COMMON SCREEN ELEMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SIDEBAR / MENU
      On the left of the Clinic Admin System, and in the slide-out menu on
      the portal. Only the sections your role is allowed to open are shown.

  TOP HEADER
      Shows the current page title, notification bell, dark-mode switch and
      your account menu (Profile, Change Password, Log Out).

  DARK MODE
      Click the sun/moon icon to switch between light and dark appearance.
      Your choice is remembered on that device.

  TOASTS
      Short messages that slide in at the corner to confirm an action
      ("Saved!", "Scheduled!") or warn you about a problem.

  BROADCAST BANNER
      A coloured strip across the top of the screen carrying a system-wide
      announcement from the Super Admin (for example, scheduled downtime).

  SEARCH AND FILTER BARS
      Most list screens (Patients, Inventory, Invoices, Appointments) have a
      search box and filter chips. Filters combine with the search text.

                  PART II - CLIENT PORTAL (FOR PET OWNERS)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CREATING AN ACCOUNT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You have two ways to register.

  OPTION A - REGISTER WITH EMAIL
    1. Open the portal and click "Register".
    2. Fill in the form:
         - Full name
         - Email address (must be real - the verification link is sent here)
         - Mobile number
         - Address, Province, City/Municipality, ZIP code
         - Password, and Confirm password
    3. Tick that you accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
    4. Click Register. A confirmation screen tells you a verification link
       was sent to your email.
    5. Open your inbox and click the verification link. If you cannot find
       the email, check the Spam or Promotions folder.
    6. Return to the portal and log in.

  OPTION B - SIGN UP WITH GOOGLE
    1. Click "Sign up with Google" and choose your Google account.
    2. Because Google does not supply your address or phone, the system asks
       you to complete a short profile form (phone, address, province, city,
       ZIP).
    3. Submit it and you are signed in.

  NOTES
    * The province and city lists are Philippine location data; pick your
      province first, then the city list is filtered for you.
    * Mobile numbers are entered in the Philippine format (11 digits).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. LOGGING IN / FORGOT PASSWORD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TO LOG IN
    1. Go to the portal and click "Log In".
    2. Enter your email and password, or use "Continue with Google".

  IF YOU FORGOT YOUR PASSWORD
    1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen.
    2. Enter the email address on your account and submit.
    3. Open the reset link sent to your email.
    4. Type your new password twice and save. You can now log in.

  IF YOU CANNOT GET IN
    * "Account pending deletion" - you requested deletion of your account.
      A recovery screen appears with an option to cancel the deletion within
      the grace period.
    * "Account suspended / deactivated" - the clinic has restricted the
      account. Contact the clinic directly; the on-screen message usually
      states the reason.
    * "Maintenance" screen - the system is briefly unavailable. The page
      checks automatically every 15 seconds and returns you when it is back.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. THE OWNER DASHBOARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After logging in you land on the Dashboard. It shows:

  * A greeting and quick summary of your account.
  * YOUR PETS - a card for each registered pet with photo, species and
    breed. Click a card to open that pet's profile.
  * UPCOMING APPOINTMENTS - the next scheduled visits with date, time,
    service and status.
  * MEDICAL SUMMARY CARD - the most recent clinical summary recorded by the
    clinic for your pet.
  * PET CARE RECOMMENDATIONS - general care tips matched to your pet's
    species and life stage.
  * Shortcuts to Register Pet and Book Visit.

  The portal menu contains: Dashboard, Register Pet, Book Visit,
  Visit History, Notifications and Invoices.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. REGISTERING AND MANAGING PETS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TO REGISTER A PET
    1. Click "Register Pet" in the menu.
    2. Fill in the details:
         - Pet name
         - Species (Dog, Cat, etc.)
         - Breed (the list depends on the species you chose)
         - Sex
         - Date of birth (the system computes the age)
         - Colour / markings
         - Weight and weight unit
         - Known allergies, if any
    3. Optionally upload a photo of your pet.
    4. Click Save. The pet now appears on your dashboard.

  TO EDIT A PET
    Open the pet's profile and click Edit, change the fields and save.
    Weight and photo are the fields most often updated.

  PET PROFILE
    The pet profile page shows the pet's demographic details, the medical
    summary shared by the clinic, and that pet's appointment history.
    Medical records are entered by the clinic - owners can view what the
    clinic has shared but cannot edit clinical entries.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. BOOKING AN APPOINTMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    1. Click "Book Visit".
    2. A calendar is shown. You can switch between Month, Week and Day views
       using the arrows and view buttons. Coloured dots mark existing
       appointments:
             grey    = Pending
             green   = Approved / Scheduled
             red     = Cancelled or Declined
             blue    = Completed
    3. Click the date you want. Past dates cannot be selected.
    4. Choose the PET the visit is for.
    5. Choose one or more SERVICES (for example Consultation, Vaccination,
       Grooming). Only services the clinic has marked Active are listed.
    6. Choose a VETERINARIAN if the selected service requires one. Services
       that do not require a doctor will not ask for this.
    7. Pick a TIME SLOT. Slots run from 08:00 to 17:00 in 30-minute steps.
       Slots already taken by an active appointment are hidden, so double
       booking is prevented. For today, past times are not offered.
    8. Add NOTES describing the reason for the visit or anything the vet
       should know.
    9. Submit. The appointment is created with status PENDING.

  WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
    The clinic reviews the request and either approves or declines it. You
    receive a notification, and the appointment status on your screen
    updates live. If declined, the clinic's reason is included.

  TO CANCEL OR RESCHEDULE
    Open the appointment and cancel it, then book again at the new time, or
    contact the clinic directly. The booking screen states this as well.

  EMERGENCIES
    Do not book through the portal for emergencies. Call the clinic and
    bring your pet in immediately.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. VIEWING VISIT HISTORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  "Visit History" lists every appointment for all your pets, newest first,
  with the date, time, pet, service, assigned vet and status. Click any row
  to see the full details, including the notes you submitted and the
  clinic's response.

  Once a visit is marked COMPLETED, you may be invited to leave a review.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. VIEWING INVOICES AND RECEIPTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The "Invoices" page lists the bills the clinic has issued to you, showing
  the invoice number, date, total amount and payment status.

  Open an invoice to see the itemised services and products, discounts, VAT
  and the amount paid. A fully paid document is presented as a RECEIPT and
  displays the payment method; an unpaid or partly paid one is presented as
  an INVOICE.

  Payment statuses you may see: Draft, Finalized, Partially Paid, Paid.

  Payments themselves are settled at the clinic - the portal does not
  process online payments.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. NOTIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The bell icon shows a badge with the number of unread items. The
  Notifications page collects:

    * appointment approvals, declines and reminders
    * vaccination and follow-up reminders
    * invoices sent to you by the clinic
    * messages or warnings sent by clinic staff
    * system announcements

  You can mark all as read, dismiss a single item, or clear the list.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. LEAVING A REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  After a completed visit the portal offers a review form. Give a star
  rating and, optionally, written feedback about your experience.

  Reviews are moderated: clinic staff approve them before they can appear
  publicly, and may feature selected reviews on the clinic's public page.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. MANAGING YOUR PROFILE AND ACCOUNT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  EDIT PROFILE
      Update your name, phone number, address and profile photo.

  CHANGE PASSWORD
      Enter your current password and the new one twice.

  DEVICES
      You can review the devices signed in to your account and sign a device
      out (or sign out everywhere) if you no longer recognise it.

  DELETE ACCOUNT
      Requesting deletion places the account in a pending-deletion state
      rather than erasing it immediately. During the grace period you can
      recover it by logging in and choosing to cancel the deletion; the
      clinic can also cancel a pending deletion on your behalf.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
15. USING THE VIRTUAL ASSISTANT (CHATBOT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The chat bubble in the corner of the public pages opens the AutoVet
  assistant. Tap one of the suggested questions or type your own. It can
  answer questions about:

      * clinic location and branch address
      * contact numbers and email
      * the services offered
      * clinic hours
      * how to book an appointment
      * general pricing guidance
      * what to bring for a first visit
      * what to do in an emergency

  The assistant answers from a fixed set of clinic information. It does not
  give medical advice and cannot access your account or book for you.

16. LOGGING IN AS STAFF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    1. Open the Clinic Admin System address and enter your email and
       password. Accounts are issued by your Clinic Admin.
    2. If you are logging in with a password that was reset for you, the
       system sends you to the Change Password screen first. Set a new

    3. You land on the Dashboard. The menu shows only the modules your role

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
17. DASHBOARD OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The Dashboard is the clinic's daily control screen. It contains:

  METRIC CARDS
      Headline figures such as the number of patients, clients, today's
      appointments and sales totals.

  TODAY'S SCHEDULE
      Appointments for the current day with the pet, owner, service, time
      and status; and a list of upcoming appointments.

  SALES SUMMARY
      Revenue for the current period.

  INVENTORY AI STATUS / FORECAST INSIGHTS
      A summary of what the forecasting module currently predicts: which
      items are trending toward Low Stock, and when the last forecast ran.
      A "Run Forecast" action recalculates predictions on demand, and a
      toast tells you when the run has finished.

  ANALYTICS CHARTS
      Compact charts for consumption and clinic activity.

  RECENT NOTIFICATIONS
      The latest internal alerts - low stock warnings, new bookings,
      expiring items, new client registrations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
18. ANALYTICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The Analytics page collects the clinic's reports:

  SALES
      Revenue summary over time, top-performing services, and transaction
      volume.

  PATIENTS
      Species distribution, registration trends, and demographics
      (age groups, sex, size categories).

  INVENTORY
      Stock levels, consumption trends and the low-stock report.

  TRANSACTIONS
      Trends and statistics across invoiced transactions.

  Use the date range and category controls on each card to narrow the view.
  Charts are for reading on screen; the patient and medical reports in the
  Patients module are the ones designed for printing/PDF export.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
19. PATIENT RECORDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The Patients module holds owners (clients) and their pets (patients).

  ADDING A PATIENT
    1. Go to Patients and click Add Patient.
    2. Enter the OWNER details first - name, mobile number, address,
       province and city. The system checks for a duplicate mobile number
       and warns you if an owner with that number already exists, so you can
       attach the pet to the existing client instead of creating a double.
    3. Enter the PET details - name, species, breed, sex, date of birth,
       colour, weight and allergies. Optionally upload a photo.
    4. Save. The pet appears in the patient list.

  THE PATIENT LIST
      Search by pet name or owner, and filter the list. Each row opens the
      patient's profile.

  THE PATIENT PROFILE
      Four tabs:

      OVERVIEW
          Demographics (breed, sex, age, colour, weight, date of birth),
          last visit, next due date, allergies and clinical notes, plus the
          owner's contact card. You can export a Patient Medical Summary
          as a PDF from here.

      MEDICAL RECORDS
          Every consultation recorded for the pet. A record captures the
          chief complaint, findings, diagnosis, treatment plan and the
          products or medicines used. Veterinarians create and edit these
          entries. You can export a single medical record as a PDF, or the
          pet's entire medical history as one report.

      APPOINTMENTS
          The pet's booking history with dates, services, notes and status.

      INVOICES
          Every invoice issued for this patient.

  EDITING AND OWNER TOOLS
      From the patient list and profile you can edit the pet, edit the
      owner's details, open the owner's full profile, and - where your role
      allows - send the owner a warning message about conduct such as
      repeated no-shows.

  RECORDING PRODUCT USE
      When medicines or consumables are used on a patient, record them
      against the visit. The system deducts the quantity from inventory
      automatically, so stock levels and the forecast stay accurate.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
20. APPOINTMENTS AND WALK-INS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The Appointments module manages both portal bookings and in-clinic
  registrations. A Calendar view of the same data is also available.

  HANDLING A PORTAL REQUEST
    New bookings arrive with status PENDING and appear immediately (no
    refresh needed).

      APPROVE  - confirms the slot. The owner is notified and the status
                 becomes Approved/Scheduled on their screen.
      DECLINE  - you must type a reason. The reason is sent to the owner.
      REMIND   - sends the owner a reminder notification about the visit.
      COMPLETE - marks the visit as finished after the pet has been seen.
                 A completed visit is what makes the review request and the
                 billing step available.

  CREATING AN APPOINTMENT AS STAFF
    Use the Add/Schedule action, choose the owner, pet, service(s), vet,
    date and time, and save.

  REGISTERING A WALK-IN
    1. Open the Add form and switch on "Walk-in".
    2. Choose NEW OWNER or EXISTING OWNER.
         - New owner: enter name, phone, address, city and province. As you
           type the phone number the system checks for duplicates and warns
           you if that number already belongs to a client.
         - Existing owner: search and select the client; their pets are
           offered for selection.
    3. Enter the pet (name, species, breed) or pick an existing one.
    4. Tick "Emergency" if the case is urgent so it is prioritised.
    5. Submit. The owner, pet and visit are created together in one step.

  DOUBLE-BOOKING PROTECTION
    A time slot already held by an active appointment is not offered again.
    Cancelled and declined appointments release their slot.

  VET SCHEDULES
    Availability is driven by the vet schedules configured under
    Maintenance -> Vet Schedule.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
21. INVENTORY WITH AI FORECASTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The Inventory module tracks medicines, consumables and retail products by
  BATCH, so each batch keeps its own batch/lot number and expiry date.

  READING THE LIST
      Each row shows the item name, batch number, current stock level with
      its unit of measure, category, and the AI status. Filter chips let you
      show All, Low Stock, or Out of Stock, and the search box finds items
      by name.

      Colour coding:
          green  = healthy stock level
          amber  = LOW STOCK - the forecast predicts it will run out soon
          red    = OUT OF STOCK - zero units left, reorder immediately

  ADDING AN ITEM
    1. Click Add Item.
    2. Enter the item name, category, unit of measure, unit cost/price,
       reorder point, batch/lot number, quantity and expiry date.
    3. Save.

  RECEIVING STOCK
    Use "Receive" on an existing item to log a new delivery. Each delivery
    is recorded as its own batch row with its own quantity and expiry date,
    so the clinic can track which batch expires first.

  VIEWING AN ITEM
    Opening an item shows its details, its batches, and its transaction
    history - every receipt, deduction and adjustment with dates, so stock
    movement is auditable.

  AUTOMATIC DEDUCTION
    Stock is deducted automatically when products are used on a patient or
    added to an invoice. Manual counting is not required for normal use.

  THE AI FORECAST
    The forecasting module fits a linear regression over each item's
    cumulative consumption history and projects it forward. For each item it
    produces:

        * a predicted depletion date (when stock is expected to hit zero)
        * a suggested reorder timing
        * a forecast status (for example Low Stock)
        * a CONFIDENCE LEVEL derived from how well the trend fits the data:
              High   - strong, consistent consumption pattern
              Medium - moderate fit
              Low    - erratic or sparse history; treat as a rough hint

    You can view an item's current forecast, its saved forecast and its
    forecast history, and you can ACCEPT a forecast to act on its
    recommendation. Forecasts can be recalculated for all items from the
    Dashboard's Run Forecast action.

    IMPORTANT: the forecast is decision support. It suggests when to
    reorder; it never orders anything by itself, and a Low confidence result
    on an item with little history should be checked against your own
    judgement.

  EXPIRY MONITORING
    The system checks for approaching expiry dates and raises notifications
    so near-expiry batches can be used or pulled first.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
22. INVOICES AND BILLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The Invoices module turns a visit into a billing document.

  CREATING AN INVOICE
    1. Open Invoices and start a new invoice, selecting the client and pet
       (usually from a completed appointment).
    2. Add LINE ITEMS - services performed and products/medicines used.
       Each line has a quantity and unit price; the subtotal updates as you
       type. Products added here are deducted from inventory.
    3. Apply a DISCOUNT if applicable. Choose PERCENTAGE or a FIXED AMOUNT.
       The discount is capped so it can never exceed the subtotal.
    4. VAT of 12% is applied to the discounted amount automatically.
    5. Review the total due.

  SAVING AND STATUS
       DRAFT          - still being edited; nothing is committed.
       FINALIZED      - the invoice is issued.
       PARTIALLY PAID - the amount paid is greater than zero but less than
                        the total due. The system sets this automatically.
       PAID           - the amount paid covers the total due. Set
                        automatically once payment is recorded.

  RECORDING PAYMENT
    Enter the amount paid and the payment method. The status is promoted to
    Partially Paid or Paid on its own based on the amount - you do not set
    it manually.

  PRINTING AND SENDING
    Export the document as a PDF. An unpaid document prints as an INVOICE;
    a fully paid one prints as a RECEIPT and shows the payment method. You
    can also send the invoice to the client so it appears in their portal
    Invoices page and notifications.

23. REVIEWS AND FEEDBACK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  (Veterinarian and Clinic Admin only.)

  Reviews submitted by owners after completed visits arrive here for
  moderation. For each review you can:

      APPROVE  - allow it to be shown publicly
      FEATURE  - highlight it among the public reviews
      DELETE   - remove it

  Only approved reviews are visible on the public side of the portal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
24. AI CLINICAL SUPPORT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  This module is available only to accounts where AI features have been
  enabled. If it is switched off for your account, the page reports that the
  feature is not enabled.

  HOW TO USE IT
    1. Open AI Clinical Support and select the patient.
    2. Type the presenting SYMPTOMS in your own words (a short description
       is enough; the field accepts up to 1000 characters).
    3. Submit.

  The system builds a profile for the AI from the pet's species, breed, age,
  weight, sex, recorded allergies and a summary of its five most recent
  medical records (chief complaint, diagnosis and treatment plan), then
  returns supporting suggestions to consider.

  LIMITS - READ THIS
    The output is reference material for a qualified veterinarian. It does
    not diagnose, does not prescribe, and must never replace clinical
    examination and professional judgement. Always verify against your own
    findings before acting.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
25. CLIENT NOTIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  From this area staff can communicate with clients:

    * Send a message to a client manually.
    * Send an invoice to a client.
    * Send a warning to an owner (for example, for repeated no-shows).
    * Review the client notification history - what was sent, to whom and
      when.

  Internal staff notifications (low stock, new bookings, expiring items)
  have their own history page.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
26. MAINTENANCE / SETTINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  (Veterinarian and Clinic Admin only.) Settings is organised into tabs.

  CLINIC PROFILE
      Clinic name, address, contact details and branding shown on documents
      and in the portal.

  MASTER DATA
      The reference lists the rest of the system depends on: inventory
      categories, service categories, units of measure, pet size categories
      and weight ranges. Keep these tidy - they populate the dropdowns
      everywhere else.

  SERVICE MANAGEMENT
      The clinic's services. For each service set the name, category, price,
      whether it is ACTIVE (only active services can be booked by owners)
      and whether it requires a veterinarian (which controls whether the
      booking form asks the owner to pick a vet).

  SPECIES & BREEDS
      Maintain the species list and the breeds under each species. These
      drive the pet registration forms on both applications.

  USERS / ROLES
      Create clinic user accounts, assign roles (Staff, Veterinarian,
      Clinic Admin), deactivate accounts and reset a user's password. A user
      whose password was reset is required to change it at next login.

      This area also covers PORTAL USERS - the owner accounts. You can
      suspend, deactivate or reactivate a portal account. A suspended or
      deactivated owner is signed out and shown an explanatory screen.

  VET SCHEDULE
      Set each veterinarian's working days and hours. Schedules can be added
      one at a time or in bulk. Appointment availability follows these
      schedules.

  SYSTEM & AI PREFERENCES
      System-wide switches, including maintenance mode and the AI feature
      toggles.

  SYSTEM AUDIT LOGS
      A searchable record of who did what and when - logins, record changes,
      deletions and administrative actions.

  BACKUP & RESTORE
      Create a backup on demand, list existing backups, download one, delete
      one, or restore the system from a backup. RESTORING OVERWRITES CURRENT
      DATA - take a fresh backup first and be certain before you proceed.

  ARCHIVE & RECOVERY
      Deleted records are archived rather than destroyed. Browse archived
      items by type, RESTORE one back into active use, or permanently delete
      it. Portal accounts pending deletion are listed here too, and their
      deletion can be cancelled on the owner's behalf.

30. STATUS DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  APPOINTMENT STATUSES
      Pending    - submitted by the owner, awaiting clinic action.
      Approved / Scheduled / Confirmed
                 - accepted by the clinic; the slot is reserved.
      Declined   - refused by the clinic; a reason is given to the owner.
      Cancelled  - called off; the time slot is released.
      Completed  - the visit took place. Enables billing and review.

  INVOICE STATUSES
      Draft          - being prepared, not yet issued.
      Finalized      - issued to the client.
      Partially Paid - some payment received, balance outstanding.
      Paid           - fully settled; prints as a receipt.

  INVENTORY STATUSES
      In Stock     - healthy level, no action needed.
      Low Stock    - the forecast predicts depletion soon; reorder.
      Out of Stock - zero units; immediate reorder required.

  FORECAST CONFIDENCE
      High   - the consumption trend fits the data strongly.
      Medium - moderate fit; usable but verify.
      Low    - weak or sparse history; treat as a hint only.

  PORTAL ACCOUNT STATES
      Active            - normal use.
      Suspended         - temporarily blocked by the clinic.
      Deactivated       - blocked; contact the clinic.
      Pending deletion  - deletion requested; recoverable during the grace
                          period by the owner or the clinic.

31. TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  I DID NOT RECEIVE THE VERIFICATION EMAIL
      Check Spam/Junk and Promotions. Confirm the address was typed
      correctly. If it is wrong, register again with the correct address or
      ask the clinic for help.

  THE TIME SLOT I WANT IS NOT LISTED
      It is already booked, it is in the past, or it falls outside the
      bookable window (08:00-17:00) or outside the selected vet's schedule.
      Try another slot or another day.

  I CANNOT SELECT A VETERINARIAN
      The service you selected does not require one. This is normal.

  A PAGE SAYS "ACCESS DENIED"
      Your role is not permitted to open that section. Ask your Clinic Admin
      if you believe you should have access.

  THE SYSTEM SHOWS A MAINTENANCE SCREEN
      Maintenance mode is on. The portal re-checks automatically every 15
      seconds and returns you when the system is available again.

  I WAS SIGNED OUT UNEXPECTEDLY
      Your session ended, your password was reset, or your account status
      changed. Log in again; if it repeats, contact the clinic.

  STOCK NUMBERS LOOK WRONG
      Open the item and review its transaction history - every movement is
      listed with its source. Remember that products added to invoices and
      recorded against patients are deducted automatically, so do not deduct
      them a second time by hand.

  A FORECAST LOOKS UNREALISTIC
      Check its confidence level. Items with little or irregular
      consumption history produce Low confidence forecasts. Adding more
      usage history improves accuracy over time.

  IMAGES ARE NOT LOADING
      Reload the page. If a specific pet or user photo stays broken,
      re-upload it from the edit form.

32. DATA SAFETY, PRIVACY AND BACKUPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ACCESS CONTROL
      The system enforces role-based access. Owners can only ever see their
      own pets, appointments and invoices. Staff see only their clinic's
      data.

  AUDIT TRAIL
      Administrative actions and record changes are written to the audit
      log, so changes can be traced to a user and a time.

  ARCHIVING
      Deleting a record archives it instead of destroying it, and it can be
      restored from Archive & Recovery.

  BACKUPS
      Clinic Admins should take regular backups from Maintenance -> Backup
      & Restore, and download a copy off the system. Always take a backup
      immediately before restoring one.

  ACCOUNT SECURITY - GOOD PRACTICE
      * Do not share accounts; every user should have their own login so the
        audit log stays meaningful.
      * Change any password that was reset for you at first login.
      * Review your signed-in devices and remove ones you do not recognise.
      * Log out on shared or public computers.

  PRIVACY
      The portal's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy pages describe how
      client and pet data are handled. Owners may request deletion of their
      account from their profile.
