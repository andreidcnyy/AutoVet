                            PART I - INTRODUCTION

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ABOUT AUTOVET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AutoVet is a veterinary clinic management system made up of three connected
parts:

  (a) THE CLIENT PORTAL - a website where pet owners register, add their pets,
      book appointments, view invoices and leave reviews.

  (b) THE CLINIC ADMIN SYSTEM - a dashboard where clinic staff manage patients,
      appointments, medical records, billing and inventory.

  (c) THE AI FORECASTING MODULE - a prediction engine that estimates when each
      inventory item will run out, so stock can be reordered before it is gone.

The three are linked. Booking in the portal creates an appointment the clinic
sees immediately. Finalizing an invoice deducts stock. Deducted stock becomes
the data the forecast learns from.

WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES NOT DO
  - It does not diagnose animals. The AI Clinical Support tool (Section 25)
    offers reference suggestions only; a licensed veterinarian decides.
  - It does not accept online payments. Invoices are produced and recorded in
    the system; payment itself happens at the clinic.
  - It does not order stock automatically. The forecast recommends; a human
    places the order.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. USER ROLES AND WHAT EACH CAN DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
There are five roles.

  SUPER ADMIN    Platform level. Manages clinics, clinic accounts, system-wide
                 broadcasts and other super admins. Can view data across all
                 clinics.

  CLINIC ADMIN   Full control of one clinic: staff accounts, services, pricing,
                 inventory, reports, settings.

  VETERINARIAN   Clinical privileges - writes medical records and diagnoses -
                 plus normal clinic operations and settings access.

  STAFF          Front desk. Appointments, patients, invoicing, inventory and
                 analytics. Cannot open Settings or Reviews, and does not see
                 the sales/revenue card on the dashboard.

  OWNER          A pet owner using the Client Portal. Sees only their own pets,
                 bookings and invoices.

SCREEN ACCESS BY ROLE (Clinic Admin System)

  Screen                  Super Admin  Clinic Admin  Veterinarian  Staff
  ----------------------  -----------  ------------  ------------  -----
  Dashboard                    -            Yes           Yes       Yes
  Analytics                    -            Yes           Yes       Yes
  Patients                    Yes           Yes           Yes       Yes
  Appointments                Yes           Yes           Yes       Yes
  Inventory                    -            Yes           Yes       Yes
  Invoices                     -            Yes           Yes       Yes
  Reviews & Feedback           -            Yes           Yes        -
  AI Clinical Support          -            Yes           Yes       Yes
  Maintenance / Settings       -            Yes           Yes        -
  Clinic Management           Yes            -             -         -
  System Broadcasts           Yes            -             -         -
  Super Admins / Logs         Yes            -             -         -

  A dash means the item does not appear in that role's sidebar. Super Admin
  works from the platform screens rather than the clinic screens; Patients and
  Appointments remain reachable for support purposes.

Access is enforced on the server, not merely hidden in the interface. Opening a
restricted address directly produces a "Forbidden" screen or an empty result,
never another clinic's data.

3. SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS & ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - A current web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
  - An internet connection.
  - No installation. Both systems run in the browser.

  Client Portal        - the portal web address supplied by the clinic
  Clinic Admin System  - the admin web address supplied by the clinic

Both work on phones and tablets as well as desktop computers. A dark-mode
toggle is available in the portal.

ACCOUNTS

  - Clinic staff accounts are created for them by a Clinic Admin or

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

                     your role may open are listed.

  FILTER CHIPS       Buttons such as All Items / Low Stock / Expiring / Expired
                     that narrow a list to one category.

5. CREATING AN ACCOUNT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
There are two ways to register.

  A. EMAIL REGISTRATION
     1. Open the portal and choose Register.
     2. Fill in your details and choose a password.
     3. Submit the form. The system sends a verification link to your email.
     4. Open the email and click the link.
     5. You may now log in.

     The verification link expires and can only be used once. If it expires,
     register again or use Forgot Password.

  B. GOOGLE SIGN-IN
     Choose the Google option on the login or register screen and pick your
     Google account. No separate password is created.

COMPLETING YOUR PROFILE
After your first sign-in the system may ask you to complete your profile
(contact number and address) before you can book. This is required so the
clinic can reach you about an appointment.

REGISTRATION LIMITS
For security, registration, password reset and login attempts are rate limited.
If you try too many times in quick succession you will be asked to wait about a
minute before trying again. This is normal and not an error with your account.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. LOGGING IN / FORGOT PASSWORD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LOGGING IN
  1. Open the portal and enter your email and password, or use Google sign-in.
  2. You arrive at your Dashboard.

FORGOT PASSWORD
  1. Choose "Forgot password" on the login screen.
  2. Enter your email address.
  3. The system emails you a reset link.
  4. Open the link and set a new password.

  The reset link expires and works only once. The system never emails you your
  existing password - it cannot, because passwords are stored scrambled and
  cannot be read back by anyone, including clinic staff.

ACCOUNT STATES YOU MAY ENCOUNTER
  ACCOUNT BLOCKED     The clinic has suspended portal access for your account.
                      Contact the clinic directly.
  PENDING DELETION    You asked for your account to be deleted and the waiting
                      period has not finished. You may still be able to cancel
                      the request from this screen.
  MAINTENANCE         The system is briefly unavailable while the clinic
                      performs maintenance. Try again shortly.

7. THE OWNER DASHBOARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Dashboard is your home screen. It brings together:

  - Your registered pets, with quick access to each pet's profile.
  - Your upcoming appointments.
  - Recent notifications from the clinic.
  - A medical summary card for your pet's recent clinical history.
  - Pet care recommendations.
  - Any clinic-wide announcement, shown as a banner across the top.

From here you can move to booking, your pets, invoices and notifications.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. REGISTERING AND MANAGING PETS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADDING A PET
  1. From the Dashboard choose Add Pet.
  2. Complete the form. The system records:
        - Name
        - Species and breed (chosen from the clinic's lists)
        - Date of birth / age group
        - Sex
        - Colour
        - Weight and weight unit
        - Size category
        - Allergies
        - Current medication
        - Notes
        - A photo (optional)
  3. Save. The pet now appears on your Dashboard.

Species, breeds, size categories and weight ranges are maintained by the
clinic. If your pet's breed is not listed, choose the closest match and note
the exact breed in the Notes field, then mention it at your visit.

VIEWING A PET
Open the pet from your Dashboard to see its profile, including its recorded
details and medical summary.

EDITING A PET
Open the pet and choose Edit. Update the details and save.

WHY WEIGHT AND SIZE MATTER
Some services are priced by the pet's size category or weight range, so keeping
these current helps the clinic quote you accurately.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. BOOKING AN APPOINTMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Choose Book Appointment.
  2. Select the pet.
  3. Select the service you need.
  4. Select a date and an available time.
  5. Add any notes for the clinic.
  6. Submit.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
  - Your booking is created with the status PENDING.
  - The clinic sees the request immediately on their dashboard.
  - You receive an "Appointment Received" email confirming the request arrived.
  - When the clinic reviews it, the status becomes APPROVED or DECLINED and you
    are emailed either way.
  - If it is declined, the clinic must supply a written reason, which you can
    read on your Appointments screen.

AVAILABILITY AND DOUBLE BOOKING
Available times come from the veterinarians' working schedules as configured by
the clinic. The system checks the slot on the server before saving, so two
people cannot take the same slot. If a slot disappears while you are filling in
the form, it was taken first - choose another.

IMPORTANT: a PENDING booking is a request, not a confirmed appointment. Please
wait for the APPROVED email before travelling to the clinic.

10. VIEWING APPOINTMENTS AND VISIT HISTORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Appointments screen lists your bookings with their current status
(see Section 32 for what each status means).

For a declined appointment, the clinic's written reason is shown with it.

Your pet's clinical history - what the vet found, the diagnosis and the

11. VIEWING INVOICES AND RECEIPTS

  - Open an invoice to see the itemised services and products.
  - Invoices can be downloaded as a PDF for your records.
  - A DRAFT invoice is still being prepared by the clinic and may change.
    FINALIZED and PAID invoices are settled records.

Payment is made at the clinic. The portal does not take card payments.

12. NOTIFICATIONS

The Notifications screen collects messages from the clinic - appointment

  - Unread items are marked; opening one marks it read.
  - Important notices from the clinic may also appear as a pop-up warning when

  - Clinic-wide announcements appear as a banner on your Dashboard.

Appointment emails are sent automatically for: appointment received,

  1. Open the review invitation from your Dashboard or Notifications.
  2. Give a rating and write your comments.
  3. Submit.

Reviews are read by the clinic under Reviews & Feedback. Approved reviews may

14. MANAGING YOUR PROFILE AND ACCOUNT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
From your profile you can:
  - Update your name, contact number and address.
  - Change your password.
  - Update your profile photo.
  - Request deletion of your account.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Requesting deletion starts a grace period rather than removing everything at
once. During that period the account is marked pending deletion and you can
still contact the clinic to stop it. This delay exists because the clinic is

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
15. USING THE VIRTUAL ASSISTANT (CHATBOT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The portal includes a built-in assistant that answers common questions about
the clinic - services, hours, booking, and how the portal works.

HOW IT WORKS
It matches your question against a prepared set of topics, allowing for minor
spelling mistakes. It is a structured assistant working from a fixed list of
subjects. It is NOT a conversational AI, it does not read your pet's records,
and it cannot give medical advice.

If it cannot answer, contact the clinic directly.

  - Transaction statistics for the last 30 days
  - Inventory consumption over the last 6 months
  - Current inventory stock summary

Use these to spot seasonal patterns, busy periods and which products are moving

19. PATIENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Patients screen is the clinical record system.

FINDING A PATIENT
Search by pet name or owner name. The search is indexed, so it stays fast as
the list grows. You can also filter by species.

PATIENT PROFILE
Opening a patient shows:
  - Pet details: species, breed, date of birth, sex, colour, weight, size
    category, allergies, current medication and notes.
  - Owner details: name, phone, email and address.
  - Medical history.

MEDICAL RECORDS

It captures:
  - Chief complaint
  - Findings
  - Diagnosis
  - Treatment plan
  - Notes
  - Follow-up date and time

Writing medical records is a clinical action, reserved for Veterinarian and
Clinic Admin roles.

DELETION
Deleting a patient hides it from lists but retains the record, because billing
and treatment history must remain auditable. Recovery is possible from
Settings > Archive & Recovery.

20. APPOINTMENTS AND WALK-INS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Appointments screen lists bookings from the portal and appointments created
at the clinic.

PROCESSING A BOOKING REQUEST
  - APPROVE   Confirms the appointment. The owner is emailed automatically.
  - DECLINE   Requires a written reason of at least 10 characters. The reason is
              stored and shown to the owner, and a decline email is sent.
  - COMPLETE  Marks the visit as finished once the patient has been seen.
  - REMIND    Sends the owner a reminder message for the appointment.

Approve, Decline, Complete and Remind are restricted to Clinic Admin,
Veterinarian and Super Admin roles.

WALK-INS
Clients who arrive without booking are registered through the walk-in flow.
Walk-in appointments are flagged as such and deliberately bypass the time-slot
check, because a walk-in has no pre-booked slot. Flagging them keeps reporting
honest: booked and unbooked visits remain distinguishable.

NEW-REQUEST INDICATOR
The sidebar shows a count of items needing attention, so a new booking is not
missed during a busy clinic.

22. INVENTORY WITH AI FORECASTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW STOCK IS ORGANISED - READ THIS FIRST
Stock is tracked in BATCHES, not as a single running total per product. Each
delivery is its own batch with its own expiry date, supplier and cost. Batches
of the same product share the product's code.

This matters because medicines expire. When stock is used, the system draws
from the OLDEST batch first and moves to the next when it is exhausted. This is
called FIFO (first in, first out) and it mirrors what staff do physically -
use the stock closest to expiring before it is wasted.

THE INVENTORY SCREEN
Each row is a PRODUCT showing its TOTAL stock across all its batches. Click the
arrow at the left of a row to expand it and see the individual batches, listed
oldest first. The top batch in that list is the one the next invoice will draw
from.

SUMMARY CARDS
  TOTAL STOCK ITEMS   Number of distinct products. Receiving a new delivery of
                      an existing product does NOT increase this number - it
                      increases that product's total stock instead.
  LOW STOCK ALERTS    Number of products the forecast flags as running low.
  EXPIRING PRODUCTS   Number of BATCHES expiring within 30 days.
  EXPIRED PRODUCTS    Number of BATCHES already past their expiry date.

  Expiring and Expired are counted per batch on purpose: an expired batch is
  one physical box to pull off the shelf.

RECEIVING STOCK
  1. Find the product and choose Receive.
  2. Enter quantity, batch number, and optionally lot number, expiry date, cost
     price, selling price, supplier and remarks.
  3. Save.

  A new batch is created and the product's total rises by the quantity
  received. A stock-in transaction is recorded. Receiving stock is restricted
  to Clinic Admin, Veterinarian and Super Admin.

ADDING A NEW PRODUCT
Use Add Item for a product the clinic has never stocked before. Use Receive for
another delivery of something you already stock.

AI FORECASTING
For each product the system predicts when stock will run out.

  HOW IT WORKS
  It studies how much of the item has actually been dispensed over time,
  calculates the daily usage rate, and projects the date the stock will fall
  below the minimum level. It learns only from real dispensing recorded when
  invoices are finalized. Stock corrections and data imports are deliberately
  excluded, because they are not consumption.

  CONFIDENCE LEVELS
  Every forecast carries a confidence score:
     HIGH    - usage is steady; the date is dependable
     MEDIUM  - a usable trend with visible irregularity
     LOW     - erratic usage; treat the date as a rough guide

  NEW ITEMS
  A product needs at least 3 completed transactions before a trend can be
  produced. Until then it shows "Insufficient Data" together with a progress
  percentage and the number of further transactions required. The system
  deliberately shows this rather than inventing a forecast from sample data.

  WHEN IT RUNS
  Forecasts refresh automatically every night. A Clinic Admin or Veterinarian
  can also trigger a recalculation from the inventory screen. Because results
  are stored, opening the screen is instant.

  WHAT IT CANNOT DO
  It assumes steady usage. A disease outbreak, a vaccination drive or a
  seasonal surge is a sudden change it cannot anticipate. It does not know
  supplier lead times or holidays. It recommends; it never orders.

EXPIRY MONITORING
The system checks for expiring stock every morning and notifies staff.

23. INVOICES AND BILLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CREATING AN INVOICE
  1. Open Invoices and create a new invoice.
  2. Select the patient.
  3. Add services and products as line items.
  4. Prices fill in automatically from the clinic's price list.
  5. Save as DRAFT while you work.

HOW PRICES ARE CALCULATED
Services are priced one of three ways:
  FIXED         One price regardless of patient.
  SIZE-BASED    Priced by the pet's size category.
  WEIGHT-BASED  Priced by the pet's weight range.
A manual price can be entered where required. The price used is stored on the
invoice line, so later changes to the price list never alter past invoices.

FINALIZING AN INVOICE - WHAT IT TRIGGERS
Finalizing is the point at which the invoice becomes a settled record, and it
does three things at once:
  1. The invoice locks to FINALIZED.
  2. Stock is deducted FIFO across batches for every consumable line marked to
     deduct on finalize. If the first batch cannot cover the quantity, the
     remainder comes from the next batch.
  3. A usage-history entry is written for each deduction, which becomes the
     training data for the AI forecast.

Because of step 2, finalize only when the visit is genuinely complete.

If stock is insufficient to cover a line, the system records the shortage
rather than silently allowing a negative stock level.

DOCUMENTS
Invoices can be produced as PDFs. Reports and summaries can also be exported
from the reporting screens.

REPORTS AVAILABLE
  - Sales: revenue summary, top services, transaction volume
  - Patients: species distribution, registration trends, demographics
  - Inventory: low stock report
  Sales, patient and inventory reports are restricted to Clinic Admin,
  Veterinarian and Super Admin.

25. AI CLINICAL SUPPORT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This screen provides reference suggestions to assist a veterinarian's thinking.

HOW TO USE IT
  1. Open AI Clinical Support.
  2. Provide the pet's profile details and describe the presenting symptoms.
  3. Submit to receive suggestions.

IMPORTANT - PLEASE READ
  - This is a SUPPORT tool. It does not diagnose and it does not prescribe.
    A licensed veterinarian makes every clinical decision.
  - It is a separate technology from the inventory forecasting. Inventory
    forecasting is a statistical model running on the clinic's own data; AI
    Clinical Support sends the information you type to an external AI service.
  - It requires configuration by the administrator. If it has not been set up,
    the screen reports that the AI service is not configured.
  - It can be switched on or off per administrator account, and under
    Settings > System & AI Preferences.
  - Because information is sent externally, do not enter client personal
    details that are not clinically necessary.

26. CLIENT NOTIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This screen is for communicating with pet owners.

  - Send a message to a client.
  - Send an invoice to a client.
  - Issue a formal warning to an owner, which appears to them as a pop-up they
    must acknowledge.
  - Review the history of what has been sent.

MESSAGE TEMPLATES
Automatic messages are built from editable templates, so wording can be changed
without a developer. The templates supplied with the system cover:
  - Appointment Reminder
  - Appointment Created (request received)
  - Appointment Approved
  - Appointment Declined

Templates use placeholders such as {pet_name}, {date} and {time}, which are
replaced with real values when the message is sent. Keep the placeholders
intact when editing.

Sending client notifications is restricted to Clinic Admin, Veterinarian and
Super Admin.

27. MAINTENANCE / SETTINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Settings is available to Clinic Admin and Veterinarian roles only. It contains
ten sections.

  1.  CLINIC PROFILE
      Clinic name, contact numbers, address and logo. These appear on printed
      documents.

  2.  MASTER DATA
      The reference lists the rest of the system draws on: inventory
      categories, service categories, units of measure, pet size categories and
      weight ranges. Changing these changes the options staff see everywhere.

  3.  SERVICE MANAGEMENT
      The service catalogue and its pricing. Set each service's pricing method
      (fixed, size-based or weight-based), its price rules, whether it requires
      a doctor, and which consumables it uses.

  4.  SPECIES & BREEDS
      The species and breed lists offered when registering a pet.

  5.  USERS / ROLES
      Create, edit and deactivate staff accounts and set each person's role.
      Passwords can be reset here.

  6.  VET SCHEDULE
      Each veterinarian's working hours. This is what generates the bookable
      time slots in the portal, so keep it current - an empty schedule means no
      slots for clients to book.

  7.  SYSTEM & AI PREFERENCES
      System-wide options including maintenance mode, cloud sync and AI feature
      toggles.

  8.  SYSTEM AUDIT LOGS
      A record of actions taken in the system and who took them, searchable by
      date and exportable.

  9.  BACKUP & RESTORE
      Create a backup on demand, list existing backups, download one, or delete
      one. A backup also runs automatically every night. Backups are kept on
      separate cloud storage rather than on the application server.

  10. ARCHIVE & RECOVERY
      Deleted records are retained for a recovery window rather than removed
      immediately. Restore a record here if something was deleted in error.

MAINTENANCE MODE
When enabled, the system shows a maintenance screen to users while work is
carried out. Remember to switch it off afterwards.

32. STATUS DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
APPOINTMENT STATUS
  PENDING     Requested by the owner, awaiting clinic review. Not confirmed.
  APPROVED    Confirmed by the clinic. The owner has been emailed.
  DECLINED    Refused by the clinic, with a written reason shown to the owner.
  COMPLETED   The patient has been seen and the visit is finished.
  CANCELLED   The appointment will not take place.

INVOICE STATUS
  DRAFT       Being prepared. Editable. No stock has been deducted.
  FINALIZED   Locked. Stock has been deducted FIFO and usage recorded.
  PAID        Settled.
  CANCELLED   Voided.

INVENTORY FORECAST STATUS
  SAFE               Stock is sufficient on current usage.
  REORDER SOON       Projected to run low within roughly two weeks.
  LOW STOCK          Predicted to run out soon, or below the minimum level.
  OUT OF STOCK       Zero units remain. Immediate reorder required.
  INSUFFICIENT DATA  Fewer than 3 recorded transactions; no trend yet.

FORECAST CONFIDENCE
  HIGH    Steady consumption; the projected date is dependable.
  MEDIUM  A usable trend with visible irregularity.
  LOW     Erratic usage; treat the date as indicative only.

STOCK/BATCH LABELS
  IN STOCK   Batch has units available.
  EXPIRING   Batch expires within 30 days.
  EXPIRED    Batch is past its expiry date. Remove it from use.

34. TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"A screen is empty or will not load."
  Check that your role may open it (Section 2). If it should be available, log
  out and back in, then refresh. If it persists, report it with the screen name
  and the time.

"My numbers on screen do not match the database."
  Deleted records and records belonging to other clinics are excluded from what
  you see, and the inventory screen counts PRODUCTS while the database stores
  one row per BATCH. Both are expected.

"Received stock did not increase the item count."
  Correct. Receiving stock increases that product's total quantity, not the
  number of products. Expand the product row to see the new batch.

"Changes are not appearing immediately."
  Live updating may have dropped. Screens refresh themselves every 10-30
  seconds and when you click back into the tab. Refresh manually if urgent.

"Too many attempts / please wait."
  Rate limiting after repeated login, registration or reset attempts. Wait
  about a minute.

"A forecast says Insufficient Data."
  The product needs at least 3 completed transactions. The percentage shown
  tells you how close it is.

"AI Clinical Support says it is not configured."
  It needs an administrator to supply its configuration and enable the feature.

"An owner cannot book - no times are available."
  Check Settings > Vet Schedule. Slots come from the vets' working hours.

"A client says they never received an email."
  Ask them to check spam. Confirm the email on their clinic record is correct,
  then resend from Client Notifications.

"Stock went negative or a shortage was reported."
  The invoice was finalized for more units than were in stock. Receive the
  delivery that is physically present, then review the affected invoice.

35. DATA SAFETY, PRIVACY AND BACKUPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT PROTECTS THE DATA
  - Passwords are stored scrambled with a method that cannot be reversed.
    No one, including clinic staff and system administrators, can read a
    user's password.
  - All traffic between browser and server is encrypted.
  - Every request must carry a valid session token; logging out destroys it on
    the server.
  - Each clinic's data is filtered at the database level, so one clinic cannot
    retrieve another's records even by manipulating a web address.
  - Actions are recorded in the audit log with the person who performed them.
  - Deleted records are retained for a recovery window rather than destroyed.
  - Backups run nightly to separate cloud storage.
  - Error details are never shown to users; they go to the system logs.

GOOD PRACTICE FOR STAFF
  - Never share an account. Everyone who uses the system needs their own, so
    the audit log means something.
  - Log out on shared computers.
  - Deactivate accounts promptly when someone leaves.
  - Only enter clinically necessary personal information into AI Clinical
    Support, which sends data to an external service.

36. KNOWN LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stated plainly so expectations are accurate.

  NO ONLINE PAYMENTS
    Invoices are produced and recorded; payment is taken at the clinic.

  NO SMS MESSAGING
    The system is built to support SMS and the connection point exists, but no
    SMS provider is connected. Notifications go by email and in-app messages.

  CLOUD SYNC IS NOT ACTIVE
    The system contains an offline-resilience mechanism: changes are queued
    durably and pushed to a central portal every minute, signed and protected
    against replay and duplication. However, this is switched OFF by default,
    the receiving endpoint is not currently connected, and the flow is one-way
    (clinic to cloud) with no conflict resolution. Do not rely on the system
    continuing to work without an internet connection in the current
    cloud-hosted deployment. If the connection drops, the system is
    unavailable until it returns.

  NO OFFLINE BROWSER MODE
    The websites need an internet connection. There is no offline mode in the
    browser itself.

  FORECASTING IS SINGLE-FACTOR
    It looks only at past usage over time. It does not account for seasonality,
    outbreaks, supplier lead times or holidays.

  CHATBOT IS RULE-BASED
    It answers from a fixed list of topics and cannot hold a conversation or
    read pet records.

  NO TWO-STEP LOGIN
    Accounts are protected by password, email verification and rate limiting.
    Two-factor authentication is not implemented.

  PERSONAL DETAILS ARE NOT INDIVIDUALLY ENCRYPTED
    Passwords are scrambled and all traffic is encrypted, and the database and
    file storage are encrypted by the hosting providers. Individual fields such
    as phone numbers are not separately encrypted within the database.

  NO MOBILE APP
    Both systems work in a mobile browser, but there is no installable app.

  CALENDAR NOT IN THE MENU
    The calendar view exists but must be reached by typing its address.
